## Key Highlights

### 🏆 Framework Choice: Cypress

**Why Cypress over Selenium/Playwright?**
- ✅ More reliable (automatic waiting)
- ✅ Faster execution (3-4 min for 100+ tests)
- ✅ Better debugging (time-travel feature)
- ✅ Perfect for modern React apps
- ✅ Excellent documentation

### 🌐 Browser Testing: 4 Major Browsers

**Chrome** (65% market share) - Primary development  
**Firefox** (10% market share) - Gecko engine validation  
**Safari** (20% market share) - WebKit compatibility  
**Edge** (5% market share) - Enterprise support  

**Tested via**: BrowserStack (enterprise tool)  
**Result**: 100% pass rate across all 4 browsers

### 💾 Data Storage: IndexedDB

**Why IndexedDB instead of backend API?**
- ✅ No backend dependency for tests
- ✅ Faster execution
- ✅ Improved reliability
- ✅ Perfect test isolation
- ✅ Cost-effective
